How is this calculated?

PulseCam evaluates deviations from your personal resting baseline rather than comparing you against a generic universal standard. Real-time deviations are normalized into standard scores (z-scores) for cardiovascular rhythm, eye-closure duration, blink cadence, and posture pitch.

These scores are synthesized using the heuristic weights above (30% heart-rate trend, 30% eye closure, 20% blink pattern, 20% head posture, plus elapsed session duration), clamped between 0 and 100, and smoothly filtered over time.

Heuristic Formulation: These weights are heuristic estimates, not clinically validated. PulseCam is designed to encourage mindful screen habits and pacing.
